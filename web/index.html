The rules we hold ourselves to

You always make the final call.

Nothing happens without you

No automatic cancelling, no emails sent for you. Drafts are text for you to review and send, and PayPal changes happen on PayPal.

The AI can't invent numbers

Every amount and date is computed first. The model only words it, and anything that doesn't match your data is thrown away.

Local and honest

Card and account numbers are redacted before anything is sent. Guesses are labelled as guesses, and you can mark "still using".
